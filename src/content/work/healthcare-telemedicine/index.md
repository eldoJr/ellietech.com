## Overview

Designed and built a telemedicine platform facilitating 85K+ monthly consultations with HD video, AI-powered symptom assessment, and seamless EHR integration.

## Challenges

- HIPAA compliance for all data at rest and in transit
- Low-latency video with adaptive quality
- Integration with multiple EHR systems (Epic, Cerner)

## Solution

WebRTC with custom SFU for scalable video conferencing. Python-based AI triage system using NLP to assess symptoms pre-consultation. End-to-end encryption with AWS KMS. HL7 FHIR APIs for EHR interoperability.
