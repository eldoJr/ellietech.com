# Why We Chose Astro for Our Website

When rebuilding our company site, we evaluated Next.js, Remix, SvelteKit, and Astro. Here's why Astro won.

## Zero JavaScript by Default

Astro ships zero client-side JavaScript unless you explicitly opt in. For a marketing site and blog, this means instant page loads and perfect Lighthouse scores out of the box.

## Island Architecture

When we do need interactivity — like our contact form or mobile menu — Astro's island architecture lets us hydrate only those components. The rest stays as pure HTML.

## Content Collections

Astro's typed content collections give us a CMS-like experience with the simplicity of Markdown files. Type-safe frontmatter, automatic validation, and zero runtime cost.

## The Result

Our site scores 100/100 on all Lighthouse metrics, loads in under 1 second globally, and is a joy to maintain.
