# Mobile-First Design Principles for 2025

Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices. Yet many teams still design for desktop first and "adapt" later. Here's our approach.

## Start with Constraints

Mobile forces clarity. Limited screen real estate means every element must earn its place. This constraint produces better designs at every breakpoint.

## Touch-First Interactions

We design for thumbs, not cursors. Minimum tap targets of 44px, generous spacing, and gesture-friendly interfaces that feel natural.

## Performance is a Feature

On mobile, performance isn't optional — it's the feature. We target sub-3-second load times on 3G connections and optimize every asset.

## Progressive Enhancement

Start with a solid mobile experience, then layer on complexity for larger screens. Never the other way around.
